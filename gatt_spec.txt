# ESP32 Smartwatch GATT Specification

This document describes the BLE GATT services and characteristics provided by the smartwatch for mobile interaction.

## Device Information
- **Device Name**: nimble-test (Default)
- **Appearance**: Generic Watch (0x00C1)

## Custom Service
- **Service UUID**: 59462f12-9543-9999-12c8-58b459a2712d
  (Bytes: 2d:71:a2:59:b4:58:c8:12:99:99:43:95:12:2f:46:59)

### Characteristics

#### 1. Step Count
- **UUID**: 59462f12-9543-9999-12c8-58b459a2712e
  (Bytes: 2e:71:a2:59:b4:58:c8:12:99:99:43:95:12:2f:46:59)
- **Properties**: Read, Write
- **Format**: uint32 (4 bytes, Little Endian)
- **Description**: Updates the step counter shown on the home screen.

#### 2. Song Progress
- **UUID**: 59462f12-9543-9999-12c8-58b459a2712f
  (Bytes: 2f:71:a2:59:b4:58:c8:12:99:99:43:95:12:2f:46:59)
- **Properties**: Read, Write
- **Format**: uint32 (4 bytes, Little Endian) - Seconds
- **Description**: Updates the progress bar and current time on the music screen.

#### 3. Media Control (Server-to-Client)
- **UUID**: 59462f12-9543-9999-12c8-58b459a27130
  (Bytes: 30:71:a2:59:b4:58:c8:12:99:99:43:95:12:2f:46:59)
- **Properties**: Notify
- **Format**: uint8 (1 byte)
- **Values**:
    - `0x01`: Play / Pause toggle
    - `0x02`: Previous Song
    - `0x03`: Next Song
- **Description**: Sent when physical buttons or touch buttons on the watch are pressed.

#### 4. General Notification (Server-to-Client)
- **UUID**: 59462f12-9543-9999-12c8-58b459a27131
  (Bytes: 31:71:a2:59:b4:58:c8:12:99:99:43:95:12:2f:46:59)
- **Properties**: Notify
- **Format**: UTF-8 String
- **Description**: Used for general status messages or alerts from the watch.
